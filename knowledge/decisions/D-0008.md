# D-0008: Shared farms, and sowing what grows near

Accepted 2026-10-06. Rusty's friends found a farming post's 16-block radius fine for one farmer but
wanted far larger farms. Rusty's idea: a shared farm, many farmers each with its own part of the
work, the others taking a part over when its farmer can't work (dead, eating, fighting a raid).
Previewed as the plan `~/.claude/plans/read-memory-notes-wiki-md-zippy-locket.md`; built as 0.9.0.

## Rusty's calls

- **Touching posts join into one farm,** over one post with a much bigger radius (which would have
  needed its storage cut down to the post's surroundings, or it would take tools from and fill every
  chest within 64 blocks).
- **Sowing:** "copy what grows near, so long as their copying does not interfere with what you
  decided is to be grown." Read as the four sowing rules below; the preview said so and he approved it.

## What the code it sits on does

Read in Serfdom 0.8.0 and checked in the NeoForge 21.1.248 sources, 2026-10-06.

- A post's area is the cube of its radius (farming 4 to 16), it takes four workers
  (`Assignment.MAX_WORKERS`), and every container of 18 slots or more in the cube is its storage.
- **Nothing divided the work between farmers on one post.** Each looked over the whole area and took
  up to 64 ripe crops nearest itself; two side by side planned nearly one route, and the second walked
  to crops the first had taken (`CropTask` skipped them). Woodcutters each took the tree nearest them.
  Both reproduced on 0.8.0 before the fix: a farmer walked toward taken wheat for 160 ticks, and both
  woodcutters went for both oaks.
- `WorkShift.stop` runs whenever the work activity ends (a meal takes it over as panic does, a raid, the
  end of the shift); a dead or unloaded villager's behaviours just stop being ticked.
- NeoForge's `CropGrowEvent` fires only for growth on a random tick (not bone meal), so a list of ripe
  crops kept from events would miss some; the area is swept instead.
- Vanilla reads a villager's schedule at most every 20 ticks (`Brain.updateActivityFromSchedule`).
- Farmer's Delight 1.3.3's tomato is a `CropBlock` (`TomatoBlock`) planted from `tomato_seeds`, young as
  a `BuddingTomatoBlock`; its rice stands in water, not on farmland.

## The rule

**A farm**
- Two farming posts (a crop job) of the same owner are one farm when their areas touch or overlap:
  along each axis at most r1 + r2 + 1 apart. Links chain. Nothing new to craft or click.
- Each post keeps its radius (4 to 16), its chests and its four workers; a worker's bed is still within
  48 blocks of its own post. A post without workers is more field. A bigger farm is more posts.

**Who works where**
- The field is cut into plots, the 8 by 8 columns of the world grid. A farmer takes a plot with work
  and holds it (`domain/Holds`): renewed every tick it walks to or works the plot, a deposit trip in
  the middle of it included; no other farmer works a held plot.
- It takes its own post's area first, nearest plot first, then the rest of the farm, nearest first,
  and chooses again after every plot.
- **Filling in:** the hold is let go at once when the plot is done, when the shift stops (a meal, a
  raid, its end), when the farmer starves, dies, or loses its job, and otherwise lapses 200 ticks (ten
  seconds) after it was last renewed. A farmer whose hold was taken while it couldn't renew it (it
  stood trading, frozen, stuck) drops the task when it works again.

**Chests on a farm**
- The harvest goes to the post nearest the farmer whose chests take something it carries, sorted as
  before within them; a tool comes from the nearest post holding one; seed from the nearest chest
  holding it.

**Sowing** (bare farmland: farmland with air above)
1. No crop is replaced and no ground tilled.
2. A spot that has grown a crop is sown only with that crop again: that is the player's decision.
3. A spot that has never grown anything copies its neighbours only when they agree: every crop within 4
   blocks at its height, growing or remembered, is one kind, and not a stem's. Where two kinds meet it
   is left for the player. Stems are never copied, so a never-planted spot within 4 of a stem stays
   bare, and the fruit keeps the ground beside its stem.
4. No spot is sown until it has been bare for a minute (`sow_after_seconds`, 60), so the player's own
   planting comes first.
- A farmer sows first with what it carries (its harvests' spare seed), then takes the seed from the
  farm's chests. With sowing the only work left and no seed anywhere, it shows "no materials".
- A crop's kind is what plants it (its pick-block item, a placeable seed).

**Woodcutters:** each holds the tree it fells, keyed by its trunk base, so two on one post fell two
trees.

**The post's screen** says "Farm: N posts, M farmers" when the post shares a farm. The payload changed:
the network version is "7".

## Settled in the build

- **A Work Post is a fence to every path that doesn't end at it.** Vanilla takes a block whose
  collision is no full block for open ground (D-0006 met it with the stall), so a farmer bound across
  the farm planned straight over a post, stood against its pole, and gave up: the own-area test's
  farmer "could not reach" the second post's wheat four times running, and with the rule switched off
  the 1a sorting test's farmer stood beside its post at the shift's end unable to reach its chests.
  D-0006 had given the post the stall's fence and taken it back because a worker's arrival at its post
  leans on reaching above the pole; a path that ends at the post (or on top of it, where ground
  navigation lifts a solid target) is still planned as vanilla plans it (`WorkerNavigation`).
- **The 1a gate test asked too much.** "Back out of the field" was z more than 1.5 past the gate; the
  farmer rests where vanilla's arrival leaves it, two blocks from its post, and its slide decides the
  last hundredths: 1.516 without the fence rule, 1.477 with it, out of the field and the gate shut both
  times. The test now asks that its whole body be past the gate block.
- **Sweeping is spread.** A sweep of a radius-16 area full of wheat took 0.9 to 2.3 ms (median, three
  runs on the busy GameTest server; a cold first one 32 ms), so a farm of nine read at once would have
  cost nine in a tick. An area is swept the first time it is read; after that the server sweeps one
  area a tick, the most overdue of those read in the last 200 ticks and swept 100 or more ago. A first
  try swept one area a tick in reading order and starved the other posts' areas: the farmer's own,
  always stale after its 100-tick wait, took the tick every time.
- **The stem rule needed no code of its own:** any stem within 4 blocks of a never-planted spot either
  makes two kinds there or is the only kind, which is never copied.

## Rejected

- **One post with a radius up to 64 and sixteen farmers** (Rusty chose touching posts).
- **Responsibilities by role** (harvester, sower, carrier): harvesting and replanting are one act at one
  block, and a farmer carries about 190 crops of wheat before a trip to the chests, so splitting roles
  would double the walking for nothing.
- **"The crop most common near it"** for a never-planted spot: it would sow wheat into a carrot patch
  wherever wheat outnumbered the carrots, against what the player planted.
- **Sowing at once:** a player tilling a new patch for another crop would find it sown with its
  neighbour's before planting it.
