# D-0007: Phase 5, raids and the base's defence

Accepted 2026-10-05. Phase 5 of the plan (part C of
`~/.claude/plans/the-following-requests-were-witty-church.md`): the spec's §10, with guns through the
Ranged Weapons protocol. Rusty took the preview's one call ("Agreed with you"). One build, as 0.8.0.

## Rusty's call

- **No launcher.** RWM's rocket launcher is a protocol weapon like any gun (class `launcher`), but its
  rockets explode, and a worker firing one at a pillager inside the base would blow holes in it and
  in the workers beside it. Workers never take a launcher; it stays in the chest. Rejected: allowing
  it with the hold-fire rule widened to the blast, since the blocks would still go.

## What the code it sits on does

Checked on 2026-10-05 in the NeoForge 21.1.248 sources, Serfdom, the Ranged Weapons protocol 1.7.0,
Armed Pillagers and Guard Villagers 2.4.11.

- **A base can already be raided like a village.** A raid starts, and a place counts as a village,
  within a section of an occupied point of interest tagged `#minecraft:village` (a bed, a job site,
  a bell), and assigning a worker's bed takes the bed's ticket. Vanilla's raid radius is 96 blocks
  (`ServerLevel.getRaidAt`).
- **Raiders already go for villagers,** and owned villagers keep vanilla's pre-raid, raid and hide
  activities and the core behaviour that sets them (`SetRaidStatus`), so an unarmed worker hides as
  vanilla's do.
- **Villagers have no attack damage attribute** (`Villager.createAttributes`), and `Mob.doHurtTarget`
  reads one. **Their brains hold no attack target** (`Villager.MEMORY_TYPES`), so vanilla's melee
  behaviours can't run on them.
- **The protocol:** `RangedWeapons.resolve(stack)` gives any gun its `fire(level, shooter, stack,
  shot)`, which spends nothing; `WeaponProfile.acceptsAmmo` says which loose rounds load it and
  `magazineItem` which magazine; `WeaponClass.LAUNCHER` marks a launcher. Armed Pillagers' fire control
  is the working pattern, but refills a pillager's gun from nothing.
- **Bullets are arrows underneath** (`ProfiledBullet extends AbstractArrow`) and hit whatever is in
  their way but their shooter.
- **Guard Villagers turns a villager into a guard** only when a player crouches and uses a sword or
  crossbow on it, which Serfdom already takes for its own on owned villagers; what a villager holds
  never converts it.

## The rule

**Who arms:** owned villagers, hired and captive (the spec), grown, not in chains, not on their way
home, within vanilla's raid radius of a raid. Free villagers hide as vanilla's do.

**When:** from the moment the raid exists (the bell, before the first wave), through every wave,
until it is won, lost or stopped. Raids only, never other hostile mobs.

**What it takes,** from its home chest and its post's chests:
- the best ranged weapon it has ammo for (a gun, a crossbow or a bow), never a launcher, with up to
  `ammo_carried` (64) of its rounds or arrows and any loaded magazines for it;
- the best melee weapon, as a backup;
- "best" is the most damage a second; any ranged weapon with ammo comes first, since a villager is
  fragile and does better at range;
- workers sharing a chest take first come, first served; one left with nothing hides.

**Fighting:**
- It targets the raid's raiders it can see within 32 blocks, never chasing past `defence_reach` (48)
  blocks of its bed or post.
- Melee: villagers get a fist's attack damage (1, as a player's), so an iron sword hits for 6, at the
  weapon's own speed.
- Bows and crossbows: a player's full draw (20 ticks) or a crossbow's charge (25), with the arrows it
  carries.
- Guns: a fire control like Armed Pillagers', reloading only from the rounds or magazines it carries.
- It holds fire while a villager, player, golem, guard or pet is within a block of its line of fire.
- Out of ammo: its backup melee weapon; with none, it hides.
- Armed in a raid, it neither panics, hides, eats nor shops.

**After:** each weapon and the leftover ammo go back into the chest they came from; failing that its
home chest, else the ground at its post. Worn armour stays on. What it carries is saved on it, so a
reload or an unload mid-raid loses nothing, and a defender that dies drops it all.

**Settings:** `defence` [on], `defence_reach` [48], `ammo_carried` [64].

## Settled in the build

Built 2026-10-05 as 0.8.0.

- **Magazines don't hold, through the protocol.** Ranged Weapons Mod's magazines are its own: it
  registers no `AMMO_STORE` capability for them, and no gun's profile names a magazine item (every
  RWM gun's `magazine` is empty). So a worker reloads a gun only from loose rounds the gun accepts,
  and magazines stay in the chest. Reading them would mean reaching into RWM's internals; the clean
  fix is RWM registering its magazines as protocol ammo stores, a change to RWM of its own.
- **A defender's weapon is not a tool.** `KeepBed` puts a worker's hand away whenever it is not at
  work (D-0001: the tool lives in the worker's record between shifts), and took the sword out of a
  defender's hand a few ticks after it armed. `Workers.stash` now leaves a weapon the defence put in the
  hand alone; death drops it as vanilla drops a held item (always, its drop chance set to 2 while
  armed), and a zombie's conversion drops it with what the defender carried.
- **Arrows are aimed for their speed.** A skeleton lifts its aim a fifth of the distance, for arrows at
  1.6 blocks a tick; at a full draw's 3.0 every arrow flew over a pillager's head. A defender lifts by
  what gravity takes over the flight (0.05 blocks a tick a tick).
- **Melee weapons are a tag,** `#serfdom:defence/melee`: `#c:tools/melee_weapon` (swords, axes, a mace,
  a trident), swords and axes. A woodcutter's axes in its post's chests count, and go back after.
- **Arrows are any of `#minecraft:arrows`,** tipped ones as they are; a defender's arrows can be picked
  up, since they came from the chest.
- **Nobody trades with a defender:** a trade ends by emptying a villager's hand (vanilla's
  `ShowTradesToPlayer`), which would have lost the weapon.
- **An arming trip finishes before the fight,** chests nearest first; what feeds its ranged weapon comes
  from every chest that holds some. Another worker may take a planned weapon first; then it goes
  without.
- **Putting back waits** until nothing more pressing holds it (vanilla's celebration after a won raid,
  sleep, a meal, a trade), then goes chest by chest.
- **A defender that leaves its owner drops what it took** where it stands, as its tool does: set free,
  freed by the law or escaping, a villager gets vanilla's brain back, and nothing would ever put the
  owner's weapons back. Found by the mutation pass: the "a free villager musters" mutation could not
  fail, since a free villager's brain has no defence, and asking where the rule could matter found this.
- **A raid outlives a failed test,** over its 96 blocks: under a mutation, the next batches' workers
  mustered and two workshop tests failed. Every batch's setup (`Yard.hour`) now stops any raid the
  defence tests started.

## Rejected

- **A launcher in a worker's hands:** Rusty, above.
- **Arming for any hostile mob:** the spec arms for raids only.
- **Vanilla's melee behaviours:** villagers' brains hold no attack target; the defence keeps its
  own, as the shopping trip does.
