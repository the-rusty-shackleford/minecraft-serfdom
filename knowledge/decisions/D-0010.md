# D-0010: Every villager's navigation floats, as vanilla's does

Accepted 2026-10-06, on Rusty's go; built as 0.9.2 (0.9.1 whole, plus this).

## What was seen

Rusty, on the box running 0.8.0: "For a while, one of the farmers seemed to get stuck in the water in
the farm", then "Pathfinding struggles in water in general".

## What it was

`VillagerMixin` gives every villager `WorkerNavigation` at the end of its constructor (D-0001, for the
gates; D-0008, for the post). Vanilla's constructor has just set two things on the navigation it made:
`setCanOpenDoors(true)` and `setCanFloat(true)` (`Villager.<init>`, NeoForge 21.1.248's sources).
`WorkerNavigation` set the first and not the second, so every villager, free or owned, planned as a
mob that can't swim: `WalkNodeEvaluator` starts its path from the bottom of the water, walks the
bottom, and drops a water node to the floor below it; `GroundPathNavigation` follows from the feet,
not the surface. The brain's `Swim` (kept in the worker's core) held it at the surface all the same,
so the path and the body pulled two ways.

Reproduced before the fix: a worker following its owner across a channel five wide and two deep (the
only way over) was still in the water after 600 ticks, against the far bank at the surface, never
climbing out; and a free villager's navigation read `canFloat()` false. Two one-deep cases, a farm's
water hole on the worker's straight line and a worker standing in one, passed before the fix: a hole
one deep traps nobody.

## Decision

`WorkerNavigation`'s constructor sets `setCanFloat(true)`, as the navigation it replaces had it. The
two one-deep tests stay, as guards that floating, which makes vanilla's water malus count, still takes
a worker round a farm's hole and out of one.

## Not done

- The navigation is still swapped after the constructor, so anything else a mod sets on a villager's
  navigation in its constructor, or vanilla sets in a later version, is lost the same way. Making the
  navigation in `createNavigation` instead would keep it; it was left as it is because nothing else
  was found set there in 1.21.1, and the change would touch every villager's construction.
