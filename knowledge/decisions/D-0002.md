# D-0002: Phase 1b, the cook and the blacksmith

Accepted 2026-10-04. This is the second half of phase 1 (D-0001's split): two more jobs for the
Work Post, each keeping a stock list filled. Rusty said "go" on the revised preview.

## Rusty's calls

- **"He should use whatever station … allows him to make things."** That answered whether the
  cook uses a crafting table. The first preview asked it and he rejected the question. A job is
  a set of stations, and its list takes anything those stations make.
- **"Anything made at a crafting table, or anvil, or furnace… think about what a blacksmith makes
  and what he needs to make it."** This rejected a gear-only blacksmith. The blacksmith makes
  anything his stations make, and makes first whatever it needs.
- **Two questions from the first preview were withdrawn, and the role settles them:**
  - What the blacksmith smelts unasked: raw metal.
  - What either job burns: coal, charcoal and coal blocks, and charcoal made from logs when
    they run low.
- **Guns at the weapons workbench.** The plan (part C) kept guns from the blacksmith, but only
  because gun assembly had left the crafting table. Under Rusty's rule the workbench is one more
  of his stations, so he makes guns, parts and magazines there. This was stated in the preview
  he said go to. He can still keep guns to players.

## What the code it sits on does

Checked on 2026-10-04.

- **RWM 2.12.0.** Guns, parts and magazines use `rangedweaponsmod:assembly`, a recipe type of its
  own over `CraftingInput`.
  - Shaped and shapeless alike, `getIngredients` keeps the pattern's empty cells, and `matches`
    judges a laid-out grid.
  - Ammunition is ordinary `crafting_shaped`.
  - RWM requires Metals and Materials, whose steel is `c:ingots/steel`.
- **Farmer's Delight 1.3.3.**
  - **Cooking pot:** six input slots, a meal display, a container slot (7) and an output slot
    (8). It cooks only while `isHeated()`, and a meal leaves through the container slot.
  - **Pot handler:** its item handler takes the inputs from above and the container and output
    from the sides.
  - **Recipes:** `farmersdelight:cooking` (ingredients, an output container, a cook time) and
    `farmersdelight:cutting` (one ingredient, a tool such as `c:tools/knife`, results with
    chances).
  - **Stove:** it cooks campfire recipes, `placeFood`, and pops them out as a campfire does.
- **Vanilla 1.21.1.**
  - **Melting gear:** `iron_nugget_from_blasting` and its siblings melt iron and gold tools and
    armour. A smith left to smelt whatever he finds would melt his own swords.
  - **Burn time:** a blast furnace or a smoker burns fuel at half a furnace's duration and cooks
    in half the time, so one coal does eight items in each.
  - **Anvil:** a material repair mends a quarter of the item's durability per unit, and a use has
    a 12% chance of wearing the anvil.
- **NeoForge 21.1.248.** `c:raw_materials` is raw copper, gold and iron, plus any mod's raw
  metals.
- **The pack.** Its other mods add no cooking or smithing station. Voidsteel and Galestone add
  only crafting, smelting and blasting recipes. Create's machines are not a villager's tools.

## The rule

**Jobs.** A job file names its target kind `workshop`, its stations, and its duties:

| Job | Stations | Duties | Faster |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cooking | crafting table, furnace, smoker, campfire (and Farmer's Delight's stove), cooking pot, cutting board | charcoal | butcher |
| Blacksmith | crafting table, furnace, blast furnace, smithing table, anvil, weapons workbench | charcoal, raw metal, repair | armorer, toolsmith, weaponsmith |

**The stock list.** It lives on the post:
- up to 9 rows of "keep N of X", N from 1 to 999, in priority order;
- X is anything the job's stations make;
- a row is short by N minus what the post's chests hold, minus what is on the way;
- on the way means in a station this post loaded, or in another worker's hands;
- a row never uses up what another row keeps.

**Planning a row.** This is Warehouse Manager's planner (its D-0012 and D-0013), ported with a
station on every rule:
- The worker looks at everything that makes the item, at every station he has, and at everything
  that makes what that needs, to a depth of 8.
- Nothing being made is spent to make its own ingredients.
- Rules for one item are tried by yield, then by station (crafting first, then blast furnace,
  smoker, furnace, campfire, pot), then by id.
- Smelting and campfire rules never take an item that has durability.
- The plan uses only the stations in the area that a worker can use. If it fails and every station
  would cover it, the row lacks a station. Otherwise it is short of what the plan names.
- The worker does the first step of the plan that can run now:
  - its inputs are in the chests;
  - a station of its kind is free;
  - fuel is stored if the station burns it;
  - a knife is held or stored if it is a cutting board.

**Stations.** A worker loads only an empty station, and collects only what his post loaded. The
post saves that record. A station holding anything else belongs to a player and is not his. A
pot or campfire counts only while lit or heated.

**Fuel.** It comes from the chests: `#serfdom:fuel`, which is coal, charcoal and coal blocks.
The worker loads only as much as the load needs, after what is burning and what is already in
the slot.

**The order of a worker's choices:**
1. Collect a station whose load is done, or one stalled with no fuel left.
2. The rows, in order.
3. Charcoal, when the chests hold fewer than 8 fuel or a row waits on fuel. Up to 8 logs
   (`#minecraft:logs_that_burn`) at a time, burning charcoal if any is left, else bootstrapping
   with 2 logs under 3.
4. Raw metal (blacksmith): `#c:raw_materials` from the chests into a free furnace. Silk-touched ore
   blocks are left for a player to fortune.
5. Repair (blacksmith): the most worn item in the chests whose material is stored, mended at a
   free anvil:
   - only whole quarters, so no material is wasted;
   - no XP, since a villager has none;
   - the anvil wears as it does for a player;
   - two items are never merged.
6. Nothing: with no icon when every row is met or on its way. Otherwise the first lack shows,
   in this order: no tool (a knife), no station, no fuel, no materials.

**Working.**
- **Inputs:** a task fetches its inputs from the chests, carried in the worker's 8 slots, walks
  to the station, and does it.
- **Crafting time:** a craft takes 2 seconds, faster by the profession bonus. The worker swings
  and his profession's work sound plays.
- **Outputs:** what he makes or collects, and leftovers such as buckets and bowls, go back to the
  chests at once (a workshop job keeps nothing between tasks), sorted as D-0001 sorts.
- **Several workers:** up to four on one post claim the station they use and what they are
  making, so none makes what another already is.

**The Work Post screen** gets a Stock tab:
- the rows, each with what is in stock against the target and why it is stuck;
- a picker with a search box over everything the job can make, computed on the client from the
  synced recipes.

**Needs.** A new icon, "no station", goes between "no tool" and "no fuel".

## Rejected

- **A gear-only blacksmith, or a cook without a crafting table:** Rusty, above.
- **The spec's smelting filter.** The list covers it: "keep 64 glass" smelts sand.
- **Burning wood freely.** Only coal, charcoal and coal blocks burn. Logs are made into charcoal
  when fuel runs low, and only then is one burnt to start it.
- **Farmer's Delight's placed skillet.** It is left out of 1b. It cooks one stack held for a
  player, which a worker has no use for beside the stove.
