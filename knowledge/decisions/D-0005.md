# D-0005: Phase 3, hunger and meals

Accepted 2026-10-05. Phase 3 of the plan (part C of
`~/.claude/plans/the-following-requests-were-witty-church.md`): the spec's §2 hunger and food, and
§3's captives who "only work, eat and sleep". Rusty took the preview's one call ("Go with your
recommendation"). Buying food with emeralds needs wallets and waits for phase 4.

## Rusty's call

- **The canteen.** A worker finds ready food in its home chest first, then in the chests of any of
  its owner's Work Posts within the bed-to-post reach (48 blocks) of its bed, nearest post first.
  The spec says the home chest only. But 1b's cook fills its own post's chests, so under the spec
  every house would be stocked by hand. With the canteen, the cook's kitchen feeds the base.
  Ingredients for a dish still come from the home chest only.

## What the code it sits on does

Checked on 2026-10-05 in the NeoForge 21.1.248 sources and the pack's Farmer's Delight 1.3.3.

- **Vanilla breeding** (`VillagerMakeLove`) is only in vanilla's idle package. A worker's idle
  hours, 0–2000 and 10000–12000, are the spec's meal windows. Meal hours in the schedule would have
  ended breeding for workers, D-0003's owned children with it. So a meal takes over the worker's
  activity as vanilla's panic does, then gives the schedule back.
- **Vanilla's own villager food:**
  - Villagers keep a food level for breeding (bread 4, carrots 1).
  - `TradeWithVillager`, in idle and the meeting, throws spare food between them.
  - Our hunger is separate. A meal is carried in the worker's hands, never its 8-slot inventory.
- **Food data:** `FoodProperties` gives nutrition, effects and `usingConvertsTo` (a stew's bowl).
- **Farmer's Delight's meals carry effects.** Nourishment and Comfort are good effects, so "no
  effects", as the preview had it, would have refused every one of them. The rule refuses food with
  a harmful effect, and the tag `#serfdom:not_eaten`.
- **Its pot recipes** often take ready food themselves (carrots, potatoes); `mushroom_stew` takes
  only mushrooms and a bowl.
- **1b's station collect** takes a furnace's input back out when its output is empty; it is meant
  for a stalled load.
- **Vanilla registers a bed as a home** in a server task of its own after the block is placed. A
  bed assigned in the same task reads "not a bed".

## The rule

**Hunger** (`domain/Hunger`), saved apart from the worker's record (the `serfdom:belly`
attachment), since it changes every few seconds:
- **Scale:** 0 to 20 points. A new hire or captive starts full.
- **Drain:** 1 point per waking hour (1200 ticks), half that asleep, brought up to date every 100
  ticks. It drains only in the worker's own tick, so it pauses where the world isn't loaded and is
  never caught up.
- **Below half:** every action slows, falling straight to the floor (0.5) just above 0. This stacks
  with the profession bonus and the captive slowdown in `Pace`. Walking speed is unchanged.
- **At 0:** the worker works no more, showing the hungry icon. It never dies.
- **Who:** hired workers and captives, adults only. Children (vanilla's brain) and free villagers
  don't hunger. In chains it drains but can't eat.

**When** (`domain/Meals`):
- **Windows:** breakfast (0–2000) and dinner (10000–12000), once each, unless full.
- **Any other waking hour,** when hungry: it breaks off its shift or the meeting.
- **Never:** asleep, in chains, escaping, or without a bed.
- **Retry:** after a meal, eaten or not, it waits 600 ticks before looking again.
- **Brain:** a core behaviour (`MealTime`) drains hunger and turns the brain to the `serfdom:meal`
  activity, but not during a panic, a raid or a trade. The meal (`HaveMeal`) turns the brain back
  to its schedule when done. The shift's own stop (abandon the task, stash the tool) runs as the
  activity changes.

**What** (`domain/Menu`), chosen bite by bite:
1. **Ready food from the home chest.** That's food (fills, not refused) that no heat recipe
   (furnace, smoker, campfire) improves. The most plentiful goes first, and a worker never starts
   a bite that would overshoot by more than half its worth.
2. **Ready food from the canteen.**
3. **A dish cooked from home** at a free station within 16 blocks of the home chest: a crafting
   table (only the `#serfdom:table_meals`: bread, stews, pie, cookies), a furnace, smoker,
   campfire or stove, or Farmer's Delight's pot.
   - It makes as many as it will eat, at most four.
   - Fuel comes from the home chest. A furnace or smoker counts only if the chest holds fuel or it
     already burns.
   - It waits by the station. A furnace is emptied only once its input is cooked or it has stalled.
4. **Raw food,** eaten raw, when no free station's recipe takes it.
5. **Nothing:** it shows the hungry icon.

**Where:**
- **The home chest** is the storage (18 slots or more) nearest the bed, within 8 blocks.
- **Leftovers** (a dish not eaten, bowls) go back to the home chest at the end, or to the ground.
- **A dish left cooking** when the meal is cut short stays in the station.

**Shown:**
- **Eating:** the food in the worker's hand, vanilla's eating sound and crumbs, 32 ticks a bite.
- **Need:** `HUNGRY` comes after no bed, since a bed is the fix for a worker that can't eat, and
  before the rest. Its icon is an empty bowl, drawn by `art.py`.
- **Worker Screen:** a Hunger row of the HUD's ten drumsticks, or "Doesn't hunger". The panel is
  239 tall and fits 688×288.

**Settings:** `hunger` [on], `hunger_per_hour` [1.0], `hungry_floor` [0.5].

## Rejected

- **Meal hours in the schedule.** They would have ended breeding for workers.
- **The home chest only,** as the spec has it: Rusty, above.
- **Refusing food with any effect,** as the preview had it: it refused Farmer's Delight's meals.
- **Cooking from the canteen's ingredients.** The cook keeps that stock for its list.
- **Children hungering.** They don't work, so hunger would only be a chore.
