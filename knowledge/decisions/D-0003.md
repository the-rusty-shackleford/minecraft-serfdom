# D-0003: Phase 2a, capture and captives

Accepted 2026-10-04. Phase 2 of the plan (part C of
`~/.claude/plans/the-following-requests-were-witty-church.md`) is split as phase 1 was:

- **2a**, this decision: capture, captives and the ownership rules.
- **2b**: equipment slots and the villager armour layer. It is mostly art judged by photo, so it
  does not hold 2a back.

Escape, the captive's slowdown and its humming are captive rules from the spec's §3, so they come
with capture. Rusty said "Sounds good" to the preview with the calls below.

## Rusty's calls

- **Captives never summon iron golems and do not count toward cat spawns.** The spec says no
  owned villager summons. Rusty kept golems for hired workers in D-0001 so that a growing base
  protects itself; that stands. A captive costs a chain where a hired worker costs 8 to 40
  emeralds, so captives counting would make a pen of them a free iron farm. Captives never
  meet, so only the panic summon was in question.
- **The owner of a village bought through Village Deed, and the players he trusts, commit no
  crime capturing there.** Village Deed already makes them commit no Thief crime in their
  village, and a fine would be paid to his own village. Anyone else may not capture there at
  all, as with hiring (D-0001).

## What the code it sits on does

Checked on 2026-10-04 in the NeoForge 21.1.248 sources, the Thief 1.2.4 jar, the box's Thief
config, and the source of Village Law 1.0.0 and Vanilla Wheels 1.10.1.

- **Thief.**
  - `Crime.HEAVY.commit(level, criminal, pos)` returns its `Outcome`: whether anyone was
    punished, and the witnesses.
  - It finds witnesses by Thief's rules. On the box that is up to 34 blocks, and always within 5.
  - It costs every witnessing villager −150 reputation and posts `CrimeCommitedEvent`.
  - On the box a crime counts only inside `#thief:protected` structures
    (`crime_only_in_protected_structures = true`), and a Hero of the Village commits none.
- **Village Law.**
  - Its `Reports` turns a crime over Thief's guard threshold, in a village and seen by an officer,
    into a case, or adds the crime to the open one.
  - `Law.move` posts `CaseSettledEvent` with FLED when a case becomes BANISHED, and with PAID when
    it becomes CLEARED. A case can settle twice, FLED and then PAID.
  - The event names the player and the village, not the crimes. Nothing public says whether a
    player has an open case; `Docket` is internal.
- **Vanilla Wheels.**
  - Only an `Animal` boards as cargo. That check is in `canAddPassenger`, the seat count, the
    cargo count, where a passenger stands, loading, unloading and the client's rider pose.
  - Loading needs a lead in hand. Unloading is a crouch with a lead at the open doors; a crouch
    with an empty hand at a door toggles the doors.
  - The trailer is the only vehicle with cargo. Its four adult slots stand 0.34 blocks above its
    floor's base, with a doorway 2.5 blocks high; a villager is 1.95 tall and 0.6 wide.
  - The trailer's body and its two part boxes cover its whole length and are pickable, so a
    click through its open doors lands on the trailer, never on a passenger inside.
- **Vanilla.**
  - A golem is summoned only through `Villager.spawnGolemIfNeeded`: from `gossip` (5 villagers)
    and from `VillagerPanicTrigger` every 100 ticks of panic (3 villagers). Only villagers whose
    `wantsToSpawnGolem` is true count, and the caller must be one.
  - `CatSpawner` spawns a village cat when more than 4 occupied beds lie within 48 blocks.
  - A villager is born in `VillagerMakeLove.tryToGiveBirth` through `getBreedOffspring`, takes
    a vacant bed within 48 blocks, and is given it in `giveBedToChild`. NeoForge's
    `BabyEntitySpawnEvent` is not fired for villagers.
  - `Villager.ageBoundaryReached` rebuilds the brain when a child grows up.

## The rule

**The chain.** The spec's cuffed chains are D-0001's chain lead. A villager with the chain on it is
**cuffed**.
- A cuffed villager does not work, trade, wander or escape. It goes where the chain's holder leads
  it, and stands still when nobody holds the chain.
- A snap past 10 blocks, its holder's death or logging out leaves it cuffed and standing, with the
  chain still on. (In 1a a snap dropped the chain.)
- Gestures on a cuffed villager:

  | Who, with what | What happens |
  |---|---|
  | Anyone, empty hand or a chain, nobody holding it | They take its chain |
  | The owner, empty hand or a chain, holding it | Uncuffed; the chain goes back to the owner |
  | Anyone else, empty hand or a chain, holding it | They let go; it stays cuffed |

- Anyone may cuff an owned villager that is not cuffed, with a chain, and lead it away. Only the
  owner uncuffs. Others may move a worker but never free, claim or reassign it.

**Capture.**
- A free adult villager with a profession (not a nitwit) is captured by holding use with a chain
  on it for 2 seconds within 2 blocks.
- At the start it screams in its own voice (hurt and "no", louder and pitched up), and it is held
  still for the hold.
- Letting go, looking off it, or moving more than 2 blocks away cancels the capture, and the
  villager is free to go.
- When the hold completes, the villager:
  - is CAPTIVE, owned by the player, for good: a captive is never hired;
  - is cuffed, with the player holding its chain;
  - gives back its old bed, workstation and bell, as hiring does (D-0001);
  - has its home village and the place it was taken recorded.
- Refused: a baby, an owned villager (the chain cuffs it instead), no profession or a nitwit, and
  a villager in a bought village whose owner has not trusted the player. Wandering traders are
  not villagers and are never captured.

**The law.**
- A completed capture commits Thief's HEAVY crime where the villager stood:
  - unseen: nothing;
  - seen only by villagers: Thief's reputation hit, as for any crime;
  - seen by an officer in a village: Village Law's summons, with the heavy fine.
- **The remedy.** Right after committing the crime, Serfdom asks Village Law whether the player
  now has an open case with the village. If so, the captive is owed to that case:
  - PAID: every captive owed to that player's case with that village is freed and walks home,
    and its chain is confiscated, not returned.
  - FLED: what the case was owed is forgotten. The player keeps the captives and the chains, and
    paying the debt later frees nothing.
- Village Law 1.1.0 gains that question as public API: `Cases.openAround(player, pos)`, the
  village the law would place a crime at `pos` in, when the player has an open case with it.

**The trailer (Vanilla Wheels 1.11.0).**
- Vanilla Wheels gets a cargo registry (`api/CargoRules`). A rule says:
  - which entities, once aboard, ride as cargo (on both sides);
  - which of them a player may load now;
  - which item leads them;
  - what happens to the tether as they board.
- Animals are its built-in rule, unchanged. Serfdom registers villagers: a villager rides as
  cargo, loads while cuffed and held by the loader, is led by the chain, and stays cuffed with
  nobody holding it as it boards.
- **Loading.** A click on the trailer with an empty hand, a lead or a chain, with the doors open,
  boards everything the player leads within 10 blocks that a rule admits, nearest first, while
  there is room (4 adults). The empty hand is new, for animals too: a player's chain is on the
  captive, not in their hand.
- **Aboard,** villagers stand in the four cargo slots and turn with the trailer, cuffed.
- **Unloading** is the crouch with a lead or a chain in hand at the open doors. Everyone aboard
  steps out behind, and villagers are still cuffed. (The spec's empty-hand unload collides with
  the empty-hand door toggle, and a click through the doors lands on the trailer, so a single
  captive can't be taken out by clicking it.)

**A captive's life.**
- **Schedule:**

  | Ticks | With a post | Without a post |
  |---|---|---|
  | 0–2000 | Idle in place | Idle in place |
  | 2000–10000 | Work | Idle in place |
  | 10000–12000 | Idle in place | Idle in place |
  | 12000–24000 | Sleep in its bed | Sleep in its bed |

  Idle in place is standing about within a few blocks of where the captive stands as it begins.
  It doesn't follow its owner, meet, chat, trade or breed. Meals come with phase 3.
- **10% slower.** Its walking speed carries a −10% modifier, and every action's time is divided
  by 0.9 as well as by the 1.25 profession bonus when that applies.
- **Golems and cats:** a captive never wants a golem (`wantsToSpawnGolem` is false), so it neither
  summons one nor counts for one. A captive's bed is left out of `CatSpawner`'s count.
- The Worker Screen shows Captive, or Cuffed, or Escaping.

**Escape.**
- At the first tick at or after 18000 of each day, a captive asleep in its bed rolls once: 5% by
  default. Only a captive whose chunk is loaded rolls, and a night missed is not made up.
- On success it picks a moment between 18000 and 20000 to get up, then walks toward its home
  village. Where no village is known, it walks toward where it was taken.
- When it is inside the village's bounds, or within 16 blocks of where it was taken, it becomes
  free: its bed and post are released, and its brain is vanilla's again.
- Anyone may cuff it on the way. A cuffed escapee is a captive again, and the escape is over.

**Humming.**
- While a captive works (a fell, a harvest, a craft), now and then it sings a short phrase of a
  slow minor work song. The voice is the villager's own ambient "hmm", played note by note at
  pitches inside vanilla's 0.5 to 2.0, so it needs no sound file.
- The phrases are ours, written in the manner of a burlak (Volga boatmen) song, not copied from one.
- It carries about 8 blocks and has a subtitle. The server config can turn it off.
- An audio clip of every phrase is rendered for Rusty to hear before it ships.

**Ownership.**
- The owner can set a worker free from the Worker Screen. Hired or captive, it becomes free and
  walks to the nearest village.
- A baby of two villagers with the same owner belongs to that owner as hired, keeps the bed it
  was born into, and can't take a post until grown. Any other baby is free, as in vanilla.
- A worker that dies, or turns into a zombie villager, drops its tool and everything it carries.
  Cured, it is a free villager.

**Settings** (server config, defaults in brackets): capture hold [40 ticks], capture reach [2],
escape chance [0.05], captive slowdown [0.10], humming [on].

## Rejected

- **Captives summoning golems** and counting toward cats: Rusty, above.
- **A crime for capturing in your own bought village:** Rusty, above.
- **Clicking one captive inside the trailer to take it out.** The preview offered it, but the
  trailer's body takes every click through its doors. Unloading is the crouch with a lead or a
  chain.
- **Unloading with an empty hand,** as the spec has it: that crouch toggles the doors.
- **Reading Village Law's `Docket` from Serfdom:** it is internal. One public read in Village
  Law's API keeps the coupling to what Village Law promises.
- **Recording every witnessed capture against the village and freeing it at the next payment:**
  a capture seen only by villagers opens no case, and a later case's payment would free a
  villager taken long before.
