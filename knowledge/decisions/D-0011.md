# D-0011: A villager grips its tool, arms out of the fold

Accepted 2026-10-06, Rusty's pick of two options; built as 0.9.3 (0.9.2 whole, plus this).

## What was seen

Rusty: "they look weird holding tools, as if they're holding the sprite and not the tool itself".
Vanilla draws whatever a villager holds with `CrossedArmsItemLayer`, in `ItemDisplayContext.GROUND`:
the item's flat icon at half size, standing upright in front of the folded arms, as a dropped item
floats. Nothing grips it. Every worker holds its tool so while it works, and every defender its weapon
(D-0007 left "a weapon across folded arms" open for him).

## The options put to him

- **A, gripped at the fold:** the folded arms kept, the tool drawn as a player's third-person hand
  holds it, where the right hand leaves the fold. One client layer, no art; still a tool stuck to
  folded arms, and nothing moves as it works.
- **B, an arm out, as a vindicator's:** with a tool or weapon in hand the arms leave the fold and hang
  at the sides, the right gripping it, swinging as the villager works or strikes. A model change, the
  armour and clothes to follow, the swing to send.

Rusty: "B".

## What it is

- **Which items:** anything tiered (axe, hoe, pickaxe, shovel, sword, a knife), a trident, a mace, and
  the defence's weapon kinds (bow, crossbow, the Ranged Weapons protocol's guns and launchers). Food,
  emeralds and a trade's goods stay on the folded arms as vanilla shows them. A child's arms stay
  folded. `client/Grips`, `domain/Grip.of`.
- **The arms** (`client/ArmsLayer`): two arms pivoting where a player's do, (-5, 2, 0) and (5, 2, 0),
  each the folded arm's own 4 by 8 by 4 upper sleeve (UV 44, 22) over a 4 by 4 by 4 hand a little
  narrower than the cuff. The sleeves are drawn with the villager's skin, then its biome's and its
  profession's clothes, as the profession layer draws the body, so every skin's sleeve comes as it is,
  modded professions' included; the hand with the bare skin alone, which is skin there under every
  clothes layer. No art of ours. The folded arms are hidden for such a villager (`Dress`) and vanilla's
  item on them is not drawn (`CrossedArmsItemLayerMixin`).
- **The poses** (`domain/Grip.arms`, HumanoidModel's numbers): the arms swing with the walk, the right
  half as far and a tenth of a turn forward; a blow lifts it and brings it across, the shoulders turning
  about the middle; a bow while fighting is drawn to the eye, a crossbow or gun held to the shoulder;
  both bob with age. The item sits in the hand as `ItemInHandLayer` places it.
- **The swing plays:** vanilla advances a swing only for monsters and players, so every villager's
  `swing` (the workshop's, the meals', the defence's, since 1b) stood at its first frame for ever, on
  both sides. `VillagerMixin` advances it each tick. Woodcutters now swing as they chop (one a swing's
  length, `Job.SWING`, and one as each log falls), as they replant, and farmers at each crop they take
  and each spot they sow.
- **Aiming** is the game's own "aggressive" flag, which every client already sees: `Defend` sets it
  while it fights a live target and clears it when the defence stops.
- **What it wears follows:** the armour layer's stand-in arms take the arms' poses (a player's, so a
  chestplate's sleeve and Lucky's Wardrobe's coats sit as on a player); a captive in chains wears a band
  of the anvil's dark iron round each wrist instead of the chain across folded forearms (a chain that
  small read as a line in the booth).
- **Under the pack's Fresh Animations** (Entity Model Features 3.2.4 replacing the villager model, its
  folded arms tilted and animated with the body): the arms hang from the body part as it stands each
  frame, so they lean and bob with it; Fresh Animations' folded arms hide like vanilla's. Checked in the
  booth with the pack's jars and resource pack (`-PboothLook=fresh`).

## After the photographs

Rusty, 2026-10-06, on the manacles in photo 65: "thickness is fine but its way too dark, it should be
the same color as an iron ingot. Same with the chain lead I think." The band's texture is ours now
(`manacles` in `devtools/art/art.py`), in the iron ingot's greys (#d8d8d8 faces, a #ffffff edge, a
#a8a8a8 foot) instead of the anvil's; the chain lead's item and the links strung to the villager are
the ingot's greys too (links #d8d8d8 and #a8a8a8 alternating, where they were #a8adb8 and #4d4f57).
Asked whether the chain across a captive's folded arms (vanilla's chain block, as dark) should follow:
"all ingot grey". It is vanilla's chain model with a texture of ours (`cuff_chain`).
