# D-0001 — Phase 1: hired workers, beds, the Work Post, woodcutting and farming

Accepted 2026-10-04. Serfdom is the villager overhaul in the spec Rusty passed on
(`serfdom-mod-spec.md`), planned in five phases with hiring moved into the first. This decision is
phase 1's preview, with Rusty's answers. Phase 1 ships in two releases: **1a** is everything
below with woodcutting and farming, and **1b** adds cooking and blacksmith.

## Rusty's calls

- The name stays **Serfdom**: mod id `serfdom`, package `com.chunkworks.serfdom`.
- Phase 1 is split into 1a (woodcutting, farming) and 1b (cooking, blacksmith).
- **The chain lead** (his idea) replaces any teleport. Normal leads still fail on villagers.
  - In phase 1 the chain leads workers.
  - In phase 2 the same item used on a free villager is the capture.
- A worker restocks its trades at its Work Post, as a vanilla villager does at its workstation.
- Hired workers summon golems and count toward cats as vanilla villagers do; a growing base
  protects itself. Captives are left out, decided in phase 2.
- In a village bought through Village Deed, only its owner and the owner's trusted players can
  hire.
- The Work Post is crafted from any wooden fence and any sign, shapeless.

## What the code it sits on does

Checked on 2026-10-04 in the NeoForge 21.1.248 sources, the Thief 1.2.4 jar, Village Deed's
source, and the box's mods.

- **Village Deed** offers a village on sneak-use of any adult villager. It is a plain
  `EntityInteract` listener at normal priority that does not receive cancelled events.
- **Thief's gift** is the same gesture with a gift item in hand. Its listener cancels the event
  when it takes the gift. An empty hand is never a gift.
- **A villager's brain** is rebuilt in `Villager.registerBrainGoals`:
  - on spawn, on load (`makeBrain`), and on every profession change (`refreshBrain`);
  - with the schedule `villager_default`: idle from 10, work from 2000, meet from 9000, idle from
    11000, rest from 12000.
  Entity attachments are read in `Entity.load` before `readAdditionalSaveData` rebuilds the
  brain, so a worker's state is there when its brain is built on load.
- **`ResetProfession`**, in vanilla's core package, makes a villager jobless when it has no job
  site, a profession, 0 XP and level 1 or less. Every novice hired away from its workstation would
  lose its profession the moment its job site memory went.
- **Vanilla's core package claims workstations and beds.** `AcquirePoi` claims a workstation and
  a bed (HOME), and `AssignProfessionFromJobSite` takes a profession from a workstation. The rest
  package walks a villager without a home to the closest free bed, or to the closest village.
- **Restocking.** A villager restocks only in `WorkAtPoi`, at its own job site during work time:
  at most twice a day, at least 2400 ticks apart (`shouldRestock`, `restock`).
- **Golems.** A golem is summoned in two cases:
  - when a villager gossips and 5 villagers within 10 blocks slept in the last day;
  - when 3 villagers panic.
  Nothing else is required.
- **Leads.** `AbstractVillager.canBeLeashed` is false, so a vanilla lead is refused.
- **Inventory.** A villager carries an 8-slot `SimpleContainer`.
- **Syncing.** NeoForge 21.1.248's `AttachmentType.Builder.sync` exists, so a worker's need can be
  synced to the players tracking it.
- **The box's mods.** More Villagers' `morevillagers:woodworker` profession is there. Farmer's
  Delight 1.3.3 has rice (`RiceBlock` in water, `RicePaniclesBlock` above) and tomatoes
  (`BuddingTomatoBlock`, `TomatoVineBlock`), which are not plain crops. Create 6.0.10 is there;
  Sophisticated Storage is not.

## The rule

**Hiring**
- The hire offer appears on sneak-use with an **empty hand** of an adult villager that has a
  profession and no owner.
  - It is a green `[Hire for N]` line in chat, after Village Deed's offer.
  - N is 8 × the profession level, so 8 to 40.
  - A click within 30 seconds hires the villager. The fee is taken through Carried, from
    everything the player carries, bags included.
  - The event is cancelled after Village Deed has run, so the trade screen does not cover the
    offer. A plain right-click still trades.
- In a village claimed through Village Deed, only the owner and the owner's trusted players get
  the line.
- Hiring releases the villager's old bed, workstation and bell, so the village keeps them. The
  worker keeps its profession.
- A hired villager without a bed follows its owner on foot. The chain lead is for long trips.

**Owned villagers**
- Sneak-use on an owned villager is cancelled at high priority, so neither Village Deed nor Thief
  sees it.
  - The owner gets the Worker Screen.
  - Anyone else gets a grey "‹owner›'s worker".
- **The Worker Screen** shows:
  - profession and level;
  - status (Hired);
  - the bed, the job and the current need.
  It has three buttons: Assign bed, Assign job and Clear job. A job needs a bed first.
  - **Assign bed:** the player then has 30 seconds to right-click a free bed. The worker takes
    that bed's ticket, so no other villager can take it.
  - **Assign job:** the player then has 30 seconds to right-click a Work Post. The post must be
    within 48 blocks of the bed, in the same dimension, and hold fewer than 4 workers.
  - **Assigning a bed farther than 48 blocks from the worker's post** clears the job.
  - **Clearing the job, or breaking the post,** returns the worker to ordinary villager life at
    the base.
- **An owned villager's brain** is built from vanilla's packages, leaving out every behaviour
  that claims a workstation or bed, takes or resets a profession, or walks to a village.
  - It keeps claiming a bell, so a bell at the base gathers workers at meeting time.
  - Its schedule follows its state:
    - **no bed:** it follows its owner all day;
    - **a bed but no post:** a resident's day (vanilla's, with idle wandering kept near the bed);
    - **a post:** the worker's day, below.
- **The worker's day** (game ticks):

  | Ticks | What it does |
  |---|---|
  | 0–2000 | Idle |
  | 2000–8000 | Work |
  | 8000–10000 | Meet |
  | 10000–12000 | Idle |
  | 12000–24000 | Sleep in its bed |

  Meet falls back to idle when no bell is in reach. Hunger (phase 3) and wallets (phase 4) fill
  the idle and meet hours with needs.
- **The chain lead** leads workers, snaps past 10 blocks as a lead does, and is drawn as a chain.

**The Work Post**
- The post holds:
  - the job;
  - the radius: 4 to 16 blocks, default 8; for woodcutting 4 to 32, default 16;
  - an outline toggle;
  - up to 4 workers.
  The area is a cube of that radius around the post.
- **Storage:** every block entity in the area that exposes an item handler is the post's storage.
  - The index of those positions is rebuilt when the post loads, when a block is placed or broken
    inside the area, and at the start of each shift.
  - Contents are read only when a worker fetches or deposits.
- **Jobs are data:** `data/<namespace>/serfdom/job/<id>.json` names:
  - the tool tag;
  - a target kind and an action, from code's fixed set;
  - the radius limits;
  - the professions that work 25% faster.
  A job built from existing kinds is a data file. A new kind of target is code.

**Working**
- A worker takes its job's tool from the post's chests and keeps it in hand until it breaks, then
  takes the next.
- It carries 8 slots. It deposits when they are full and at the end of the shift.
- **An action takes the player's mining time with the worker's tool,** scaled by the profession
  bonus.
- **Restocking:** each arrival at the post restocks its trades, through vanilla's own limits.
- **Woodcutting fells a natural tree whose trunk base is in the area.** Natural means:
  - the base log stands on a `#minecraft:dirt` block and is not in the placed-log record;
  - the logs connected to it of the base's kind, by face, edge or corner (acacia and dark oak
    branch diagonally), number at most 512, none of them in the placed-log record;
  - one of its highest logs touches a natural (non-persistent) leaf. Every vanilla tree's top is
    inside its canopy, including mega spruce and jungle trees with long bare trunks. (An earlier
    draft counted leaves against logs, which those trunks fail.)

  This leaves out player builds and village houses' log pillars. Mangroves, standing on their
  roots rather than soil, are left alone. The whole tree is felled from
  the top down, its leaves are cleared and the drops collected, and saplings are replanted on
  the trunk's base spots: one, or four for a 2×2 tree. The leaves cleared are exactly the ones
  vanilla would let decay once the logs are gone: those no longer within 6 of a remaining log.
- **Farming** works existing farmland only:
  - it harvests mature crops (`CropBlock` at its maximum age) and replants one seed out of the
    harvest;
  - it takes pumpkins and melons beside an attached stem;
  - it picks Farmer's Delight tomatoes and cuts the rice panicles above the water.
- **Sorting:**
  1. a chest already holding the item;
  2. else the chest whose items share the most tags with it;
  3. else the overflow chest, the container nearest the post.
  Ties go to the container nearest the post.
- **Status icons:** one need shows above the head at a time, close up only. In priority order:
  no bed, no tool, no fuel, no materials, chest full. Work resumes by itself once the need is
  met. Fuel and materials arrive with 1b.
- **Doors and gates:** workers open fence gates and doors when pathing.
- **Placed logs:** a per-chunk record of logs placed by players. It is set on placement and
  cleared on breaking or when a sapling grows there.

## Rejected

- **Teleporting a following worker:** Rusty chose the chain lead.
- **Owned villagers never summoning golems** (the spec's §5): Rusty kept vanilla golems for
  hired workers. The rule is revisited for captives in phase 2.
- **Changing Village Deed so it skips owned villagers:** cancelling at high priority does the
  same with no change there.
